## Research details

- Design meets Industry: the official DDW programme lists 11:00–18:00 for each day, 17–25 October. The clocks change on 25 October.
- GLOW: the Eindhoven route is open 18:30–23:00 each evening, 7–14 November. Other municipalities have different hours. The photograph is from GLOW 2025 and is labelled accordingly.
- Winter Market: the organiser’s main 2026 section states 12 December, 11:00–16:00. A small header retains the previous year’s date. The 2026 date and opening time are corroborated by [Muziekgebouw Eindhoven](https://mge.nl/agenda/winter-market-2026/).
- KLEM: the official [Parktheater agenda](https://www.parktheater.nl/programma?p54_page=7) publishes 20 December, 20:30–22:00; the detail page lists the start.
- Effenaar late-night events have next-day end dates. Ages 16+ or 18+ are included where expressly published; an unspecified minimum age is not guessed.
- Events without a published finish time, or with contradictory schedules, were excluded from this initial selection.

Descriptions are concise original summaries of official listings. The source links remain available on detail pages after local edits, and clearly point to the original organiser listing. Editing or deleting here does not alter external event records.
